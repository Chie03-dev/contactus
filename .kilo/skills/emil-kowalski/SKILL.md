# Emil Kowalski — interaction & motion craft

Two companion docs:

- `animate.md` — concrete animation and transition rules.
- `apple-design.md` — interaction design principles (feedback, restraint,
  detail, state).

Read the relevant one before writing motion code. The one-line version:
**animate transform/opacity, keep it 150–300ms, use standard easing, always
respect reduced motion, and make every state change explain itself.**
